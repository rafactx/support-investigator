# Cancelling an order

Who can cancel and how depends on the order status.

## Before invoicing

Until the order is invoiced, the seller, the marketplace operator or the customer (within the self-service window) can request cancellation. The platform releases the reserved inventory and voids or refunds the payment automatically.

## After invoicing

Once an invoice exists, a plain cancellation is no longer possible. The package may already be with the carrier, and the fiscal document must be reversed. Handle these cases as a return: see "Processing returns and refunds".

## What support should check

- Current status and whether an invoice was issued.
- Payment status: a denied payment usually leads to automatic cancellation without any manual step.
- Whether the customer request arrived inside the self-service window.

## What support should not do

The investigator never cancels orders. When cancellation is the right outcome, record the evidence and hand the case to someone with permission to act.
