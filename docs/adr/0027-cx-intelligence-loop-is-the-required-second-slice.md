# The CX intelligence loop is the required second slice

After the focused Fulfillment Investigation is trustworthy end to end, the next committed slice is the CX Intelligence Loop, before adding another investigation kind. It will relate persisted, human-reviewed investigation findings to the authorized organizational context available through a customer-experience (CX) analytics platform's MCP server so Product Support can quantify recurrence, transfers, satisfaction impact and automation gaps for Product and Engineering. We deliberately keep this integration out of the first slice's synchronous investigation and policy path: making it part of case resolution would add an account-, subscription- and permission-dependent failure mode, while treating it as optional future analytics would miss the continuous-learning outcome that makes a support organization improve the product.

## Consequences

The first slice must persist privacy-safe investigation outcomes and human corrections in a form the second slice can analyze. The CX Intelligence Loop may enrich and aggregate those records, but it cannot raise evidence confidence or decide an individual investigation.
